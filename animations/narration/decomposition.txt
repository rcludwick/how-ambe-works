# Narration for the Decomposition scene (scene_decomposition.py).
# Format and conventions: see pipeline.txt.

[title]
A single voiced or unvoiced decision per frame is not enough.
Real speech is periodic in some parts of the spectrum and noisy in others, at
the same instant.

[waveform]
This is a real recording, eight kilohertz, captured through the hardware.
Half a second of speech. Nothing has been decided about it yet.

[spectrum]
Take one twenty millisecond window and look at it as a spectrum.
The peaks sit on a regular grid. That spacing is the pitch, and every peak is a
multiple of it.

[bands]
Now cut the spectrum into bands, and ask of each band separately: does this
repeat at the pitch period?
Some bands answer yes and some answer no. That is the observation the whole
codec is built on.

[model]
So rebuild it as two things at once. Voiced bands get sine waves on the
harmonic grid. Unvoiced bands get noise.
The buzz and the hiss coexist, band by band, in the same frame.

[close]
That is multi band excitation. Griffin and Lim published it in nineteen eighty
eight, and everything after it is arithmetic about how few bits the description
can be squeezed into.
